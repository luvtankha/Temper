Short description: A tiny avatar companion with private estimates for supported chats.

Full description:

Choose a companion and bring a little personality to your screen. Alex is free; premium companion looks are optional one-time purchases with all eight expressions included. There is no subscription for the core overlay.

Turn TEMPER ON from its home screen, drag the small floating character to a comfortable place, and tap for a compact panel. After one-time setup and your separate permission, TEMPER automatically analyzes recent visible plain-text messages in supported one-to-one WhatsApp chats directly on your phone. Switching supported chats clears the previous estimates and starts fresh. The panel shows estimated language signals, a cautious conversation-direction estimate and one emotional-spectrum graph. Turn OFF to stop analysis and remove the companion.

Normal chat analysis keeps text only in memory on your phone, without saving or uploading it. Optional quality feedback asks for a 1–5 rating. With separate permission and everyone’s agreement, a selected session can retain up to 64 visible turns for ten minutes; only Send rating and reviewed text uploads the shown redacted conversation segments and estimates to the publisher for quality improvement. Active feedback is encrypted and retained for up to 90 days; withdrawal and deletion controls are available. Sharing is optional and automatic chat analysis does not create feedback sessions. OFF, revoking access or closing the running app stops automatic analysis. Leaving a supported chat clears its visible context while TEMPER remains ON. TEMPER never sends messages or clicks chat controls.

The floating companion can appear in ordinary apps; Android or sensitive screens can hide overlays. Automatic estimates currently require the verified WhatsApp 2.26.37.73 portrait layout with at least three complete text messages and both sent/received roles visible. Other apps, documents, quotes, reactions and ambiguous layouts may not support analysis. English is the model's strongest language. Hinglish, sarcasm and emotion estimates can be wrong. These are estimates of visible language, not knowledge of anyone's feelings.

A one-time 125 MB public model download is needed for offline analysis. Accessibility is optional, requires an affirmative in-app disclosure covering currently visible supported chats while ON, and is used only within that scope. Display-over-other-apps permission places the floating character. The active companion has an ongoing OFF control. Android permissions and the model download are guided once; everyday activation uses the home ON/OFF button.
