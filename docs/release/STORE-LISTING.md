Short description: A tiny avatar companion with private estimates for supported chats.

Full description:

Choose a companion and bring a little personality to your screen. Alex is free; premium companion looks are optional one-time purchases with all eight expressions included. There is no subscription for the core overlay.

Activate the small floating character, drag it to a comfortable place, and tap for a compact panel. With your separate permission, TEMPER can analyze recent visible plain-text messages in a supported one-to-one WhatsApp chat directly on your phone. The panel shows estimated language signals, a cautious conversation-direction estimate and one emotional-spectrum graph.

Your chat text stays in memory on your phone. It is not saved or uploaded. Pause, leave the selected chat or revoke access to stop analysis. TEMPER never sends messages or clicks chat controls.

The floating companion can appear in ordinary apps; Android or sensitive screens can hide overlays. Automatic estimates currently require the verified WhatsApp 2.26.37.73 portrait layout with at least three complete text messages and both sent/received roles visible. Other apps, documents, quotes, reactions and ambiguous layouts may not support analysis. English is the model's strongest language. Hinglish, sarcasm and emotion estimates can be wrong. These are estimates of visible language, not knowledge of anyone's feelings.

A one-time 125 MB public model download is needed for offline analysis. Accessibility is optional, requires an affirmative in-app disclosure, and is used only for the selected supported chat. Display-over-other-apps permission places the floating character. The active companion has an ongoing stop control.
