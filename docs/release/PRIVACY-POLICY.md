# TEMPER privacy policy — deployment draft

Effective date: October 2, 2026. Publisher: **Luv Tankha**. Privacy contact: **luvtankha06@gmail.com**. Public support telephone: **+91-8178185449**. This draft describes the release candidate's actual flows. Hosting providers, operational retention and the public policy URL must be finalized before store submission.

TEMPER is a floating avatar companion with optional estimates of language in a selected supported chat. Its floating companion uses permission to display over other apps and reads no other app's content. An ongoing service notification provides a stop control. TEMPER never sends messages or automatically operates chat controls.

Optional automatic analysis requires a separate, affirmative in-app Accessibility opt-in. After you start a session, TEMPER reads up to eight fully visible plain-text turns, sent/received roles, local timestamps and a local conversation identifier to keep the selected window consistent, plus composer bounds for positioning. It excludes drafts, contact lists, unrelated screens and unseen full history. Supported layout checks can reject quoted replies, reactions, documents or ambiguous content. Normal analysis text exists only in a bounded memory window on your phone; it is never saved, uploaded or included in normal logs. Leaving the selected chat, pausing, revoking or unsupported context stops new analysis capture and clears that window. In-flight inference can finish in memory after stopping, but its result is discarded. Android Accessibility settings can disable TEMPER at any time.

Optional quality feedback is separate from normal analysis and requires its own affirmative permission plus confirmation that the contributor is 18+ and everyone whose messages are included agrees. A selected feedback session can retain up to 64 visible turns and 32 actual estimates in phone memory for ten minutes. Leaving the chat stops additions; discard, revoke or expiry clears this pending buffer. Automatic redaction is incomplete, and users must review the shown text and discard identifying/sensitive material. Only choosing a 1–5 rating and tapping Send rating and reviewed text uploads those reviewed segments, sent/received roles, visible-window ordering, actual spectrum/state/direction outputs, rating, app/model version and random session/deletion identifiers. Users are not asked to provide emotion labels. No drafts, contact identifiers, timestamps or composer positions are uploaded.

The publisher's separate HTTPS feedback service encrypts submitted records and keeps active data for up to 90 days, with expiry cleanup while running and on access/startup. Feedback is used privately to evaluate and improve analysis quality; it is not sold or published. A random deletion key links the contributor's submissions; no hardware, advertising, Google account or contact identity is collected for feedback. Text can still contain personal information, so these records are pseudonymous rather than guaranteed anonymous. Private reviewers may annotate submitted examples; user ratings and model predictions are not ground-truth emotion labels. Infrastructure receives ordinary connection metadata; actual providers/metadata retention must be finalized before enabling collection. Operators must remove temporary review/training exports within seven days and propagate deletion to any retained copies/backups. The initial deployment must have no independent data-volume backups until deletion/expiry propagation is implemented.

Stop sharing withdraws future permission and discards pending feedback. Delete my submitted feedback removes active records and retains a hashed-key revocation marker for up to 90 days to reject late uploads. An already sent request may finish; deleting after it finishes removes the active result. Delete remains available after withdrawal, and failed deletions retain the local key for retry. Delete before uninstalling because uninstall removes the key. Contact the privacy email for assistance. Previously trained model influence cannot be guaranteed to be removed immediately; future training must use fresh active records. App uninstall does not itself delete submitted feedback.

The optional public model download connects directly to Hugging Face and its download hosts. They receive ordinary connection metadata, including your network address; no chat text is sent. Verified model weights are saved in app-private storage and can be removed under Settings. Model download hosts have their own privacy policies. The English model can make incorrect emotion and direction estimates, particularly with Hinglish, sarcasm and context-dependent language; TEMPER cannot establish another person's feelings.

Premium cosmetic purchases use Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details; TEMPER never receives your card information. To validate and restore a purchase, TEMPER sends the signed purchase receipt, product identifier and signature to the publisher's HTTPS verification service. That service verifies the receipt with Google Play and acknowledges valid purchases. This can include a purchase token/order information present in Google's receipt. The service processes receipts in memory and does not intentionally persist them. The device caches only a server-signed, expiring entitlement containing product, package, a token hash and validity times. It contains no chat data. Hosting providers may process connection/access metadata according to configured operational retention. Final publisher deployment must document the chosen providers and retention here. Google Play independently retains transaction data under its policies.

TEMPER includes no advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. Optional feedback uploads only expressly reviewed selected visible segments, not unseen chat history. It does not require a TEMPER cloud account. Uninstall removes local preferences, entitlements, model files and the feedback deletion key. Purchase ownership is managed by Google Play and can be restored using the same Play account; uninstalling does not erase Google's transaction records. Pause/revoke controls stop local inspection; opted-in pending feedback follows the separate ten-minute review/discard controls. Settings can remove the offline model. For publisher-held operational records or privacy requests, contact the publisher at the email above.

The developer testing build also includes clearly marked test probes and an optional USB computer-analysis flow with a separate disclosure/opt-in. Those developer tools are absent from the consumer release UI. Do not distribute a debug APK as a store release.
