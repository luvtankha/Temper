# TEMPER privacy policy — deployment draft

Effective date: October 1, 2026. Publisher: **Luv Tankha**. Privacy contact: **luvtankha06@gmail.com**. Public support telephone: **+91-8178185449**. This draft describes the release candidate's actual flows. Hosting providers, operational retention and the public policy URL must be finalized before store submission.

TEMPER is a floating avatar companion with optional estimates of language in a selected supported chat. Its floating companion uses permission to display over other apps and reads no other app's content. An ongoing service notification provides a stop control. TEMPER never sends messages or automatically operates chat controls.

Optional automatic analysis requires a separate, affirmative in-app Accessibility opt-in. After you start a session, TEMPER reads up to eight fully visible plain-text turns, sent/received roles, local timestamps and a local conversation identifier to keep the selected window consistent, plus composer bounds for positioning. It excludes drafts, contact lists, unrelated screens and full history. Supported layout checks can reject quoted replies, reactions, documents or ambiguous content. Text exists only in a bounded memory window for analysis on your phone; it is never saved, uploaded or included in normal logs. Leaving the selected chat, pausing, revoking or unsupported context stops new capture and clears the window. In-flight inference can finish in memory after stopping, but its result is discarded. Android Accessibility settings can disable TEMPER at any time.

The optional public model download connects directly to Hugging Face and its download hosts. They receive ordinary connection metadata, including your network address; no chat text is sent. Verified model weights are saved in app-private storage and can be removed under Settings. Model download hosts have their own privacy policies. The English model can make incorrect emotion and direction estimates, particularly with Hinglish, sarcasm and context-dependent language; TEMPER cannot establish another person's feelings.

Premium cosmetic purchases use Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details; TEMPER never receives your card information. To validate and restore a purchase, TEMPER sends the signed purchase receipt, product identifier and signature to the publisher's HTTPS verification service. That service verifies the receipt with Google Play and acknowledges valid purchases. This can include a purchase token/order information present in Google's receipt. The service processes receipts in memory and does not intentionally persist them. The device caches only a server-signed, expiring entitlement containing product, package, a token hash and validity times. It contains no chat data. Hosting providers may process connection/access metadata according to configured operational retention. Final publisher deployment must document the chosen providers and retention here. Google Play independently retains transaction data under its policies.

TEMPER includes no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or chat-history upload in the consumer release. It does not require a TEMPER cloud account. Uninstall removes local preferences, entitlements and model files. Purchase ownership is managed by Google Play and can be restored using the same Play account; uninstalling does not erase Google's transaction records. Pause/revoke controls clear local inspection context; Settings can remove the offline model. For publisher-held operational records or privacy requests, contact the publisher at the email above.

The developer testing build also includes clearly marked test probes and an optional USB computer-analysis flow with a separate disclosure/opt-in. Those developer tools are absent from the consumer release UI. Do not distribute a debug APK as a store release.
